    Questions

    Before you buy

    How do I receive it?

    Instantly after checkout, as a digital download. The file is a PDF that opens on any phone, tablet, or computer, and prints cleanly on US letter paper.

    Is this medical advice?

    No. It is an educational guide compiled against published clinical guidelines and research. It does not diagnose and does not replace care from a clinician who has examined you. It is designed to be taken to your appointments, not used instead of them.

    Who wrote it?

    Sira Sculpt Editorial, compiled against the 2021 US Standard of Care for Lipedema, the 2024 German clinical guideline, Lipedema Foundation and Fat Disorders Resource Society guidance, and peer-reviewed research through September 2026. The full source list is printed at the back.

    Does it push your leggings?

    They are mentioned twice, in the compression chapter and the daily-life chapter, as one option among the garments women use, alongside the medical-grade garments many women need. The book says so on its second page.

    Is it about surgery?

    One chapter is. It reports the September 2026 randomized trial, the risks in numbers, US costs, and the documentation packet insurers ask for. Most of the book is about the daily management that every guideline puts first.
