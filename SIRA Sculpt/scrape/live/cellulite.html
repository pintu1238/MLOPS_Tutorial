    What exactly is 4D Compression?+
    It is the everyday compression Japanese women wear under normal clothes, knit into a legging instead of a medical stocking. The 3D is the raised knit texture. The fourth D is the direction of the pressure: knit firmest at the ankle, easing up the thigh, the same way medical compression is engineered.
