      Description

      A baby who only settles on you, and two hands you need for everything else.

      The Pookie Hoodie is an oversized cream sherpa hoodie with a baby-shaped front built right into it. Unzip the arc across the chest, place your baby in, zip back up. Their head peeks out, their little arms come out of their own sleeves, and their legs and feet stay wrapped in soft sherpa with closed booties. 🧸

      The part you can't see does the carrying. A padded seat sewn inside holds your baby from below, and straps run over your shoulders and cross on your back — so the weight sits on you, not on the fleece. No buckles, nothing to adjust, nothing to figure out at 6 a.m. 🤱

      Mom sizes S, M and L in a relaxed oversized cut that falls past the hips, with two zip side pockets for your phone and keys. The baby section is the same in every size and is fits babies from 3 months up to 1 year, up to 22 lb (10 kg). 📏

      It's thick, warm sherpa. Made for the house, the couch and cooler days out — not a summer afternoon. ❄️
