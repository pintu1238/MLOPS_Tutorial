Questions, answered

Comfort Can I wear these all day?
+

Yes. Cushioned footbeds and balanced heel height are tuned for commutes, office hours, and evenings out — without the stiff soles or hot spots you get from ordinary pairs.

Quality How is Solmarch different from fast-fashion shoes?
+

We use full-grain leather and suede, reinforced stitching, and finishes meant to look sharp season after season — not tired after a few wears.

Sizing Which size should I order?
+

Use the Size Guide on this page (US 5–12). Measure foot length if you are between sizes and size up for a roomier fit. Free exchanges if the size is off.

Care How do I clean and store them?
+

Wipe leather with a soft cloth, brush suede gently, and use protector spray outdoors. Store in a cool, dry place; shoe trees help leather keep its shape.

Fit Do they run true to size?
+

Most styles run true to US sizing. If you have a wide foot or plan thick socks, consider sizing up half a size where available.

Shipping When will they arrive? What if I do not love them?
+

Orders ship in 2–3 business days with tracking. Try them at home for up to 90 days — full refund if they are not right for you.
