# Girls Winter Boutique — Overview (Page 1 of 3)

**Website:** https://y9yn6k-me.myshopify.com  
**Platform:** Shopify online store  
**Theme:** Girls Winter Boutique  

## Summary

Girls Winter Boutique sells women’s winter apparel: coats, jackets, hoodies, pullovers, and dresses. The website is a full Shopify storefront with custom design and layout. Customers can browse on mobile or desktop, add items to cart, and complete purchase through Shopify checkout.

## Brand presentation

The visual identity uses a cream background, dark text, and plum and gold accents. The logo reads “Girls Winter” with “Boutique” in script below. Navigation and buttons use a clean sans-serif typeface; headlines use a serif style suited to a fashion boutique.

## Primary pages

- Home  
- Shop (all categories)  
- Individual product pages  
- About  
- Contact  
- Shipping  
- Returns & refunds  

Category links from the menu and footer lead to the relevant section on the Shop page.
