# Girls Winter Boutique — Features (Page 2 of 3)

## Homepage

The home page introduces the brand, shows five shopping categories, displays promotions and customer reviews, includes a seasonal “pick your look” campaign area with optional countdown, explains why customers can shop with confidence, and answers frequent questions. A Shop now bar is fixed to the bottom of the screen on the home page only.

## Shop page

The Shop page groups products into Jackets, Hoodies, Pullovers, Coats, and Winter dresses. A category menu at the top scrolls the visitor to the right group. Each product card links to its product page.

## Product pages

Product pages are built for conversion: multiple images, clear pricing, variant selection, promotional messaging, reviews and FAQs, and a sticky Add to Cart bar that stays visible while scrolling. Cart updates without forcing the customer to a separate cart page when using the drawer.

## Header and footer

The header provides logo, main navigation, search, account access, and cart. On mobile, a slide-out menu contains the same links. The footer lists wardrobe categories and policy links including Shipping and Returns.

## Cart

The cart drawer and cart page use the same brand colours as the rest of the site, including a clear cart heading and readable text on the header bar.
