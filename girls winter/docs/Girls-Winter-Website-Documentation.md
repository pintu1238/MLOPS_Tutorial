# Girls Winter Boutique  
Winter apparel store on Shopify  

**Address:** y9yn6k-me.myshopify.com  

---

● Girls Winter Boutique is a shop for women’s winter layers: coats, jackets, hoodies, pullovers, and dresses.  

● The range is organised around how pieces are worn in cold weather—outerwear, knits, and dresses that layer cleanly.  

● A customer can browse by category, open a product, choose size and colour where offered, see the price, add to bag, and check out.  

● Copy on the site focuses on warmth, fit, and everyday wear—not formal occasion dress codes.  

● The storefront is a custom Shopify theme (Girls Winter on the Savor base). The site is intended for public shopping; access and any launch password are controlled in Shopify Admin if needed.  

---

## Homepage  

● Every page shares the same header and footer.  

● A scrolling announcement bar runs at the top with seasonal offers (for example, winter drop and select styles on sale).  

● The header shows the Girls Winter wordmark with “Boutique” underneath, main navigation, search, account, and cart.  

● On a phone, navigation moves into a slide-out menu with the same links and brand block at the top.  

● **Hero:** Full-width boutique imagery with the brand name, script “Boutique,” and a short line on curated coats, dresses, and knits. Trust pills include rated reviews, easy returns, and US shipping.  

● **Category row:** Five cards—Jackets, Hoodies, Pullovers, Coats, and Winter dresses—each linking to the matching section on the Shop page.  

● **Customer reviews:** A horizontal row of review cards with photos, star ratings, quotes, and locations.  

● **Pick your look / sale:** A campaign block with up to five look images, a live countdown when enabled, and a button to the Shop page. Background uses the brand mauve; accent gold on the call to action.  

● **Shop with confidence:** Shipping, returns, and secure checkout explained in three columns.  

● **FAQ:** Common questions about winter dresses and sizing, expandable on the home page.  

● **Sticky Shop now (home only):** A fixed bar at the bottom links to the Shop page.  

● **Footer:** Brand line, newsletter (“Join the winter list”), wardrobe links (Winter dresses, Coats, Jackets, Hoodies, Pullovers), and details links (Shipping, Returns & refunds, About). Payment icons and copyright when turned on.

## Product details page  

● This is the page for one product.  

● On desktop, the gallery and purchase block sit in a two-column layout. On a phone, images come first, then price and choices.  

● **Gallery:** Main image, thumbnail row, and previous/next controls.  

● **Rating:** Star display and review-style messaging as configured for the template.  

● **Name, short intro, and price** sit above the purchase options.  

● **How to wear:** Short lines describe fit and layering for that product type.  

● **Variants:** Size and colour (or shade) swatches where the product has variants. Unavailable options show as sold out.  

● **Bundle / quantity offers:** Typical setup is Buy 1, a pair marked most popular (for example Buy 1, Get 1 FREE), and Buy 3 marked best offer. On multi-line offers, each line can use a different size or colour where variants exist.  

● **Add to Cart** adds the selection to the cart and opens the **cart drawer** without sending the customer to a full cart page first.  

● **Trust lines** under the button cover dispatch time, shipping, returns window, and payment methods shown by Shopify.  

● **Expandable sections** can include benefits, how to use, materials, questions, and details (shipping, returns, SKU).  

● **Story and proof blocks** explain why the piece works in winter—fit, fabric, and layering.  

● **Written reviews** and wearer-style notes appear in scrolling rows on smaller screens.  

● **Style in motion:** A row of short vertical videos (category reels) on the product template where enabled.  

● **Shop with confidence** bar repeats shipping, guarantee, and support messaging before the footer.  

● **Sticky bar:** Stays at the bottom with product name, star line, offer text, and **Add to Cart**. On phones roughly 351–749px wide, the title sits on the left and the button on the right, aligned with the header side margins.

## Shop, search, and bag  

● The **Shop page** is the main browse experience: one URL with five category sections and product cards (image, title, price, link to PDP).  

● **Collection pages** in Shopify remain available if linked elsewhere; the designed path is Shop → product.  

● **Search** opens from the header. Predictive search shows products filtered to the Girls Winter catalog where configured, with styled results and empty-state suggestions.  

● **Search results** use the theme search template.  

● The **bag** works as a **drawer** from the header and as a **full cart page**.  

● The drawer header shows **YOUR CART** and item count on a plum bar so the title stays readable.  

● The customer can change quantity, remove lines, and proceed to checkout.  

● **Checkout** is Shopify Checkout, as a guest or signed in.  

● **Accounts** cover login, order history, and addresses through Shopify customer accounts.  

● **About** and **Contact** cover the brand and enquiries (contact form).  

● **Shipping** and **Returns & refunds** are dedicated theme pages with icons and plain-language policy copy—not blank Shopify policy stubs.  

● **FAQ** on the homepage covers dress fit, sizing, and care; additional policy text can live in Admin.

## Customer path  

● Homepage or search → Shop category or product card → product details page.  

● The customer chooses size and shade, selects a purchase option if shown, adds to bag, reviews the drawer or cart page, and checks out.  

● From the homepage sale block or sticky Shop bar → Shop page → product → bag → checkout.  

● From footer wardrobe links → Shop section anchor → product → bag → checkout.
